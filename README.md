## Five checks any company can run this week

1. **DMARC.** A policy of `p=none` only reports. It does not stop anyone from sending email as your domain. `p=reject` asks receiving servers to refuse mail that fails the check.
2. **MFA.** Turn it on for email, VPN and admin accounts. Then switch off the legacy mail protocols that skip MFA.
3. **Remote access.** Look for RDP or admin panels reachable from the internet. Close them or put them behind a VPN.
4. **Backups.** Keep one copy that ransomware cannot reach, and restore from it at least once for real.
5. **Escalation.** Know who answers when an alert fires at 3 a.m., and write their number down.

## Open Grid

[Open Grid](https://github.com/Konstantinos-Chatzistavrou/Open-Grid) is a street racing sim that runs in the browser. It is open source (Apache-2.0) and non-profit.

- Realistic handling: the vehicle physics is a C++ core compiled to WebAssembly.
- Up to six players per party over WebRTC, with wheel force feedback and tilt steering on phones.
- A bot API: write a driver in plain JavaScript and race it headless.
- [Play the live demo](https://racing-game-brown-six.vercel.app/) in desktop Chrome or Edge.

Help wanted: people with a steering wheel to judge the handling, 3D artists and bot writers.
